### W1. HalpernLascoumesLeGales2014 (PARTIAL) used for a substantive concept

**Location**: Chapter 3, section 3.3, last paragraph (line 249 of the .tex):

> `\textcite{HalpernLascoumesLeGales2014Instrumentation} permettent de lire cette configuration comme une sédimentation d'instruments qui s'ajoutent, se recomposent et se spécialisent sans rupture d'ensemble.`

**Why it is a problem**: This is the only reference with PARTIAL status, meaning only the introduction of the 2014 volume was read. The concept attributed here -- "sédimentation d'instruments" -- is not a term distinctively traceable to that introduction. The synthesis for Halpern et al. 2014 discusses "inertie, conflits et recompositions" and "instruments comme traceurs de changement," but the word "sédimentation" does not appear. Worse, two paragraphs later in section 4.2, the note correctly attributes the *same general idea* to Palier (FULLTEXT, who uses "empilement" and "traceurs du changement") and to the Lascoumes & Le Gales conclusion (FULLTEXT-CIBLE, who use "glissements," "reconversions-adaptations," "recyclages"). The PARTIAL reference is doing work that fully-read references already do better, and the concept it is credited with cannot be verified.

**Sources involved**: `HalpernLascoumesLeGales2014Instrumentation` (PARTIAL), versus `palierChapitre7Instruments2005` (FULLTEXT) and `lascoumesConclusionLinnovationInstrumentale2005` (FULLTEXT-CIBLE).

**Fix**: Replace the reference or reword to use verifiable concepts. Proposed replacement (in French):

```
Ancienne version:
\textcite{HalpernLascoumesLeGales2014Instrumentation} permettent de lire
cette configuration comme une sédimentation d'instruments qui s'ajoutent,
se recomposent et se spécialisent sans rupture d'ensemble.

Nouvelle version:
Avec \textcite{palierChapitre7Instruments2005} et la conclusion de
\textcite{lascoumesConclusionLinnovationInstrumentale2005}, on peut lire
cette configuration comme un empilement d'instruments qui s'ajoutent, se
recomposent et se spécialisent sans rupture d'ensemble.
```

This removes the only substantive reliance on the PARTIAL reference. Halpern et al. 2014 remains legitimately cited in the corpus section (line 177), where it appears in a general listing of theoretical works -- a use that requires no specific concept verification.

### W3. ONPE 2024 figure slightly under-qualified

**Location**: Chapter 4, section 4.1 (line 259):

> `79 % des Français déclarent avoir réduit le chauffage`

**Why it is a problem**: The ONPE source (confirmed in synthesis) says "79 % des Français déclarent avoir réduit le chauffage **pour limiter leurs factures**." The note drops the qualifier "pour limiter leurs factures." This is a small but material omission: 79% reducing heating for *any reason* (comfort, travel, climate) would mean something different from 79% reducing heating *because of cost pressure*. The figure's analytical force in this note -- connecting energy precarity to ventilation behavior -- depends on the cost-driven nature of the reduction.

**Sources involved**: `ONPE2024TableauBord` (FULLTEXT).

**Fix**:

```
Ancienne version:
79~\% des Français déclarent avoir réduit le chauffage

Nouvelle version:
79~\% des Français déclarent avoir réduit le chauffage pour limiter
leurs factures
```
